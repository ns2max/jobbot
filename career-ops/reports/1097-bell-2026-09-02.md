**Level detected:** Titled "Data Scientist II" but scoped as Management / technical lead / "primary AI capability builder … technical guidance and direction to the broader team." - "I've run the experimentation-to-production transition end to end: my postdoc work went from streaming-data prototype to a monitored <30ms edge service."
- "I translate ambiguous asks into shipped systems and measurable value — 300% throughput, 99.5% inspection-time reduction at MAS; 74× speedup over baseline in my JAES paper."
- Position solo research leadership (EU-funded project, partner coordination, 3 pubs in a year) as evidence of driving direction without needing to be told what to build.

**If they downlevel / lowball comp:**
- Accept only if base ≥ $130K CAD with a written 6-month review tied to explicit promotion criteria (scope → title/band alignment).
- Below $115K → decline; that's below market for a lead-scoped AI role and below the candidate's floor for a non-mission-driven corporate role.
