\textbf{Re: \clRole{} -- \clCompany{} (Fox Corporation)}

Dear Hiring Team,

Your posting describes building and deploying ML pipelines end to end: data extraction, feature development, training, testing, deployment and monitoring against business goals. That is the exact pipeline shape I have owned repeatedly, most recently on the EU-funded MUSMET project at the University of Trento, where I built a full ML pipeline for streaming multi-dimensional data, acquisition through deployment with monitoring, shipping real-time inference at 14 ms and F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the baseline (JAES 2026).

Before the PhD I led ML and computer-vision system development at MAS Holdings, building real-time ingestion and ETL pipelines across geographically distributed manufacturing sites and optimizing algorithmic components for reliability at scale, the same distributed discipline a recommendation platform needs across regions and audiences. I have owned the ingest--feature--train--deploy--monitor architecture four separate times, across four data domains, each with a shipped, measured result.

I want to be direct about one gap: I have not shipped a production recommender system. The algorithm-family specifics, collaborative filtering, ranking losses, embeddings-based retrieval, are the part I would expect to ramp on quickly; the infrastructure and production discipline are not new to me, and my research is squarely in sequential-data modelling and evaluation. Tubi's scale and its free, ad-supported model make the recommendation and ranking problem unusually interesting, and I would expect to contribute on pipeline reliability and offline/online evaluation early.

I am a Canadian permanent resident based in Toronto, eligible to work without sponsorship, and available immediately. I would welcome the chance to discuss how this experience maps onto Tubi's recommendation systems work. My portfolio and publications are at \href{https://nishal.xyz}{nishal.xyz}.
