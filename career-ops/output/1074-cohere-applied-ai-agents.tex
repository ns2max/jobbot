\section*{Professional Summary}

Engineer who builds the evaluation and reliability layer under production AI systems, not just the model. At MAS Holdings, deployed human-in-the-loop inspection models on a live manufacturing line, with safety-relevant fallback paths and real production monitoring. PhD work at the University of Trento built benchmark suites (F1, ablations, latency budgets) to know precisely when a system is trustworthy versus when it fails, the same question agent evaluation asks, applied to embedded audio ML instead of enterprise workflows; the shipped system ran at 14 ms and F1 0.76. Has not shipped a consumer or enterprise LLM-agent product end to end; what it brings instead is a decade of building the observability, benchmarking and human-in-the-loop discipline that makes any AI system safe to trust in production. 10 peer-reviewed publications.
