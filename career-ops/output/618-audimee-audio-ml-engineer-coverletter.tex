\textbf{Re: \clRole{} -- \clCompany{}}

Dear Audimee Team,

I am applying for the \clRole{} position. The job description reads like a list of my last few years: generative audio modelling, feature-extraction pipelines, model quality and inference performance, and the ``bonus'' musical background, which in my case is the foundation of the whole career rather than a bonus. My published detector runs at 14 ms, F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the baseline (JAES 2026).

My generative audio work is hands-on: at McGill University I built diffusion-based and VAE-based synthetic audio generation to improve model robustness under limited labelled data, the same family of techniques behind modern singing-voice synthesis. My PhD and postdoc (University of Trento, EU-funded MUSMET) centred on real-time audio ML: designing feature-extraction pipelines (MFCC, STFT, spectral methods), training sequence models in PyTorch, and relentlessly improving quality and inference performance with baselines, ablations and honest trade-off analysis. Ten peer-reviewed publications in AES and IEEE venues, plus 9,800+ recordings of audio and MIDI datasets I designed and released publicly, and Nebula, an open-source C++17 audio-feature library.

I will be direct about the gap: I have not shipped SVS or voice-conversion models specifically, and my vocoder knowledge is from the literature rather than production. But the underlying stack, diffusion models, audio representations, quality evaluation, production integration, is exactly where I work, and I am a performing and recording guitarist (Trinity Grade 8) who evaluates model output with a musician's ear. Audimee's focus on artist-facing generative tools is where that combination pays off.

I am in Toronto, Canada, a Canadian permanent resident eligible to work without sponsorship, available immediately, with a fully flexible schedule and years of practice collaborating on European hours from EU-project work. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my experience in generative and real-time audio ML can support \clCompany{}. My portfolio, publications and datasets are at \href{https://nishal.xyz}{nishal.xyz}.
