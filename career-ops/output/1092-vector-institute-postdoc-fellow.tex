\section*{Professional Summary}

Research sits at the intersection of classical signal processing and modern deep learning, the technical-overlap pivot into broader ML research this fellowship is designed for. Brings a benchmark-first discipline (F1, ablations, latency budgets), four open-access datasets used as community benchmarks, EU-funded research (MUSMET, EIC Pathfinder), and hands-on production ML experience (MAS Holdings, Forestpin) that grounds the work in deployment realities.
