\section*{Professional Summary}

Machine learning research engineer with a PhD (University of Trento, 2025) and 10+ years across industry and academia, focused on turning imperfect, noisy real-world sensor data into validated, reproducible models. My work centres on signal processing and statistical modelling on messy data, model validation and generalization, and benchmark and ablation design that separates a real improvement from a better-looking metric. In my postdoc I designed the frozen-protocol benchmarks behind a published detector running at F1 0.76 in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4, 74$\times$ faster than the DTW baseline it replaced (JAES 2026), with a written record of what did not work. Earlier work carried the same discipline into deployed systems: financial anomaly detection on messy structured data at Forestpin, and computer-vision inspection plus a COVID-19 remote-vitals device shipped across factories and hospital wards at MAS Holdings. I diagnose production failures and distribution shift from real-world data and communicate findings to technical and non-technical audiences across 10+ peer-reviewed papers.
