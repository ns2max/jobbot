\section*{Professional Summary}

Machine learning engineer with a PhD (University of Trento, 2025) and 10+ years across industry and academia, focused on taking models from research and experimentation into scalable, production-ready systems. I have owned the full ML lifecycle end to end, from data acquisition and label-ontology design through training, evaluation and cloud-to-edge deployment with monitoring, on both a doctoral research pipeline and production manufacturing systems at MAS Holdings. My postdoctoral work built automated frozen-protocol benchmarking and model-validation pipelines around a real-time detector running inference in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4 at 31.4\% CPU, F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the DTW baseline it replaced (JAES 2026). At MAS I built real-time dataset ingestion and ETL for high-frequency IoT streams across three continents and introduced CI/CD and code review. Backed by 10+ peer-reviewed papers and four open-access benchmark datasets.
