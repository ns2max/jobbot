\section*{Professional Summary}

Applied scientist and research engineer with a PhD (University of Trento, 2025) and 10+ years across industry and academia, focused on turning ambiguous, open-ended problems into production systems end to end. My method is rigorous experimentation and evaluation: frozen-protocol benchmark suites, baselines and ablations, and a written record of experiments including negative results, so that a real improvement is distinguishable from a better-looking metric. I adapt scientific techniques to production constraints of latency, cost and reliability: in my postdoc I designed the benchmarks and the model behind a published detector running at F1 0.76 in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4, 74$\times$ faster than the DTW baseline it replaced (JAES 2026). I work across multimodal sensor and device heterogeneity, aligning audio, IMU, BCI/EEG and mixed-reality telemetry across four musicians live, and earlier integrating camera, sensor and IoT streams at manufacturing scale at MAS Holdings. My record includes 10+ peer-reviewed papers, four open-access benchmark datasets, journal and program-committee review, and supervision of two BSc thesis students.
