\textbf{Re: \clRole{}, \clCompany{} (Satellite Systems)}

\clAddressee

I am applying for the Embedded Artificial Intelligence Architect role with the Satellite Systems team. I am an ML research engineer who prototypes and deploys models on embedded targets under a hard device budget, with a PhD from the University of Trento and 10+ years across industry and academia. My most recent published result is a real-time detector running inference in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4 at 31.4\% CPU, F1 0.76, 74 times faster than the 1038 ms baseline it replaced (JAES 2026). That number came from the methodology as much as the model: a frozen-protocol benchmark suite that measured model performance, latency, CPU and memory on the target platform for every design choice, including the ones that failed and were recorded as such.

Several parts of this role are work I already do. I deploy and prototype ML on embedded targets, with Raspberry Pi and ARM embedded Linux as my production environment, and I select model architectures to fit an embedded compute, power, memory and latency budget rather than assuming the compute is free. I design simulators and rule-based generators to produce representative training data when real data is scarce: rule-based synthetic-variation generators at roughly 10k variants per pattern, plus VAE- and diffusion-based synthetic audio for low-label regimes. I create and curate datasets for training, validation and testing with frozen protocols, and I define metrics and evaluate robustness and resource utilization on device. My toolchain is Python with TensorFlow for embedded deployment and PyTorch, production C/C++17, embedded Linux, and Git with CI/CD. Network traffic prediction is a time-series forecasting problem, and sequence modelling over streaming signals (RNN, LSTM, GRU) is the core of my research, so it is a problem I am equipped to formulate and prototype.

I want to be direct about the stretch. Satellite communications and RF, and the comms-specific stack the posting names (DVB-S2X, cognitive radio, anti-jamming), are new to me; my signal-processing and DSP fundamentals transfer, but I would be learning the communications domain. This is also a step up into an architect and technical-lead role. What I bring to it is end-to-end ownership of edge-ML delivery, from acquisition through DSP front-ends, training, evaluation methodology and on-device deployment with monitoring, plus experience defining evaluation protocols and mentoring engineers and graduate students. I am ready to be the accountable architect for the AI, work a phased roadmap from ground prototypes to on-board inference, and grow into the comms specifics with the team.

MDA's AURORA software-defined satellites, and the goal of running useful AI on the platform itself, are the kind of hard-constraint problem I want to work on: the compute, power and thermal budget in orbit is exactly where my benchmark-first approach earns its keep. I am a Canadian permanent resident, eligible to work without sponsorship, available immediately, and open to relocating to the Sainte-Anne-de-Bellevue office. I am willing to obtain reliability-status security clearance and complete the Controlled Goods Program assessment. On the ITAR requirement, I am a Canadian permanent resident and a Sri Lankan citizen, so I would want to confirm early in the process how that condition applies to this role, and I am glad to complete whatever security processes are required. My publications, datasets and portfolio are at \href{\clSite}{nishal.xyz}.
