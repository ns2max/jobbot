# Machine Learning Engineer, AI Processors (Sr to Staff)

**Company:** Qualcomm Canada ULC · **Job Area:** Engineering Group > Machine Learning Engineering · **Location:** Markham, Ontario, Canada
**Pay range:** CAD 131,200.00 - 181,200.00 base + discretionary annual bonus + annual RSU grant opportunity

## General Summary

We are seeking a talented Machine Learning Engineer to join the Qualcomm AI Processor Solutions team, where you will help develop next-generation AI applications for Qualcomm Snapdragon platforms. In this role, you will work on cutting-edge machine learning algorithms and technologies spanning computer vision, multimodal AI, and agentic AI systems, and contribute to research and development of AI applications optimized for accuracy, efficiency, and real-world performance on Snapdragon-powered devices. (Replacement position.)

## Responsibilities

- Stay current with the latest advances in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and foundation models.
- Research, develop, and optimize AI algorithms for deployment on Qualcomm Snapdragon platforms.
- Design and build innovative reference applications showcasing Snapdragon AI capabilities across mobile, edge, XR, and IoT devices.
- Evaluate and balance hardware and software trade-offs to achieve optimal performance, power efficiency, and user experience.
- Support the implementation, optimization, and commercialization of AI algorithms and applications on Snapdragon devices.

## Qualifications

- Strong background in machine learning algorithms, computer vision, multimodal AI, agentic AI, or related fields.
- Proficiency with modern machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Passion for innovation and for building efficient AI solutions on edge devices.
- Strong software development skills in Python and/or C++.
- Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work effectively in a cross-functional environment.
- Familiarity with embedded development, operating systems concepts and software development principles.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Master's or PhD degree with 3+ years of relevant work experience or publications in the areas of machine learning, agentic AI, computer vision, video/image processing algorithms, NLP or audio/speech processing.
- Experience developing and deploying AI solutions in real-world applications.
- Experience translating AI/ML algorithms into software applications running on edge devices.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or related field and 4+ years of Hardware/Software/Systems Engineering experience; OR
- Master's degree + 3+ years; OR
- PhD + 2+ years.
