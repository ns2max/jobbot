\section*{Professional Summary}

Machine Learning engineer with a PhD and 10+ years across industry and academia I have exptensive experience on perception systems on resource-constrained hardware. I built and deployed computer-vision inspection pipelines for apparel manufacturing at scale, integrating camera, sensor and IoT streams into live workflows, cutting inspection time 99.5\% and lifting operational efficiency by up to 300\%. During my doctoral work, I developed a real-time detector running inference in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4 at 31.4\% CPU, F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the DTW baseline it replaced, plus a training-free method reaching 95\% detection with no training data. My work runs from data labeling and evaluation design through deployment, monitoring and CI/CD.
