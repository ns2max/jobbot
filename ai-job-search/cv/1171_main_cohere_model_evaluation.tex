\section*{Professional Summary}
Research scientist whose core contribution across a decade of applied ML work is evaluation methodology: designing the benchmark before trusting the result. At the University of Trento, built frozen-protocol benchmark suites and systematic ablations (RNN vs.\ DTW; audio vs.\ MIDI; pattern-set sizes 1/3/10) that quantified accuracy/latency/CPU trade-offs and preserved a written record of negative results, culminating in a published real-time pattern detector (14 ms inference, F1 0.76, 74x faster than its DTW baseline, JAES 2026). Earlier work (DAFx 2022) demonstrated training-free, zero-shot detection at 95\% accuracy -- a rigorous no-training-data evaluation design still cited in later benchmark work. Released four open-access datasets (9,800+ recordings, 70+ musicians), each shipped with a documented, frozen evaluation protocol rather than an informal train/test split. Combines this benchmark-design discipline with human-evaluation methodology (structured user studies, quantitative + qualitative), latency/throughput characterization (MIRaaS, IEEE IS2 2026), and hands-on multimodal, real-time, and edge-deployed ML systems work. Seeking to bring this evaluation-first rigor to assessing large-scale foundation models.
