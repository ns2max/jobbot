\section*{Professional Summary}

Research scientist in real-time multimodal machine learning, with a PhD (University of Trento, 2025) and 10+ years across industry and academia. My work fuses and models heterogeneous sensor streams under hard timing constraints: inertial, audio and mixed-reality tracking data synchronized and classified in real time, with hierarchical state machines governing transitions between behavior modes. These are the sensor-fusion, latency, behavior-modeling and safe-deployment problems of autonomy perception, applied in a different domain. My published models run inference in 14 ms on embedded hardware, F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the baseline (JAES 2026); a gesture-and-audio fusion model reaches F1 0.78 across a 500-event corpus (IEEE IS\textsuperscript{2} 2025). I have designed four open benchmark datasets, published 10+ peer-reviewed papers, and reviewed for IEEE venues.
