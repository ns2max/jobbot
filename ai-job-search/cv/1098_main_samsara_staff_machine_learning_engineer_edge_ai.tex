\section*{Professional Summary}

Machine Learning Research Engineer with 10+ years of continuous experience across industry and academia, building applied ML systems end to end: from large-scale, high-frequency sensor and operational data through model design, training and evaluation to low-latency edge deployment and monitoring. I have shipped production inference on constrained hardware since 2015, first as a Research Engineer running C++/Python engines on embedded and IoT devices across MAS Holdings factories in three continents, then through a PhD and postdoc on real-time edge ML. My published work runs audio pattern detection in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4, F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the baseline (JAES 2026); earlier industrial computer-vision work cut inspection time by 99.5\% and lifted throughput up to 300\%.
