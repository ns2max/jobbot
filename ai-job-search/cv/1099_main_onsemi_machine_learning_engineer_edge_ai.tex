\section*{Professional Summary}

Machine Learning Research Engineer specializing in real-time and on-device inference for audio, sensor and time-series data, with a PhD (University of Trento, 2025) and 10+ years across industry and academia. I build constrained ML systems end to end, from acquisition and DSP through model design, training and low-latency deployment, designing to hard latency, compute, power and memory budgets on microcontroller- and ARM-class hardware. Published work runs audio pattern detection in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4 at 31.4\% CPU, F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the dynamic time warping baseline (JAES 2026); at MAS Holdings I shipped C++/Python inference engines on embedded and IoT hardware at manufacturing scale. I am the author of Nebula, an open-source C++17 audio-feature library with per-feature ARM latency benchmarks.
