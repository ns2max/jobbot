\section*{Professional Summary}
Music technology ML research engineer with a PhD focused on real-time audio pattern detection and a research track record built almost entirely on music-content analysis. Designed and published MIRaaS, a UDP edge-to-server offload architecture for Music Information Retrieval as a service (IEEE IS2 2026) directly analogous to the content-analysis infrastructure a music/media platform runs at scale. Created and released four open music-content datasets (DoMP, DoPP, DoDP, DoDP2; 9,800+ recordings, 70+ musicians) and shipped symbolic-plus-audio music modelling (onset/beat, chroma, key/chord, MIDI). Complements this with 15+ years as a performing and session guitarist (Trinity College London Rock \& Pop Guitar, Grade 8), giving direct musical-domain intuition alongside the engineering.
