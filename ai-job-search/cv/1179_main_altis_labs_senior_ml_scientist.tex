\section*{Professional Summary}
Research scientist (PhD, Trento) whose core discipline is benchmark and evaluation rigor: frozen-protocol test suites, controlled ablations, and a written record of negative results built to withstand scrutiny before a system is trusted. Domain expertise is real-time audio and multimodal time-series ML, not medical imaging or radiomics -- that specific domain knowledge would need to be built. What transfers directly is the evaluation methodology itself (quantifying accuracy/latency/robustness trade-offs against a fixed protocol, exactly the discipline required to validate an imaging biomarker against clinical outcomes), hands-on VAE/diffusion work for limited-label regimes (medical imaging's chronic small-labeled-dataset problem), and one direct clinical deployment: a computer-vision device that digitized patient vitals across hospital wards during COVID-19, recognized by Sri Lanka's national physicians' association.

\section*{Core Competencies}
\textbf{Evaluation \& Benchmark Rigor:} Frozen evaluation protocols, controlled ablation design, statistical performance analysis, cross-method benchmarking (learned vs. classical baselines), negative-result documentation, reproducible dataset design. \\
\textbf{Small-Labeled-Data Methods:} VAE-based and diffusion-based synthetic time-series generation for limited-label regimes, training-free / low-data detection methods, transfer and fusion across heterogeneous sensor modalities. \\
\textbf{Human-Centred Evaluation:} Quantitative + qualitative user studies, structured interviews, real-world usability and adoption assessment (audience/performer studies, clinical-deployment feedback).
