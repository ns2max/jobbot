\section*{Professional Summary}
Machine learning research engineer specializing in real-time and edge audio inference under hard latency budgets. Owned the full pipeline for a published streaming audio pattern detector -- acquisition, DSP/feature engineering, training, and edge deployment -- reaching 14 ms inference on a Raspberry Pi 4 at F1 0.76 and 31.4\% CPU, a 74x speed-up over the 1038 ms DTW baseline it replaced (JAES 2026).
