\section*{Professional Summary}

Deep learning researcher and engineer specializing in real-time audio and on-device inference, with a PhD (University of Trento, 2025) whose thesis is precisely this problem: embedded real-time pattern detection on constrained hardware. I take an idea from theory to a running system, designing model architectures, feature representations and training regimes that hold up inside tight latency and compute budgets, then proving them with benchmarks and ablations. My published work runs audio pattern detection in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4 at 31.4\% CPU, F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the dynamic time warping baseline (JAES 2026). I have 10+ peer-reviewed publications, four open datasets, and an open-source C++17 audio-feature library, alongside 5+ years of prior industry experience shipping vision and signal systems at MAS Holdings.
