\section*{Professional Summary}
Signal-processing-trained ML research engineer with a PhD in real-time embedded pattern detection and a track record of shipping sensor-fusion systems under hard latency, compute, and power constraints. Foundation in time-frequency signal processing (S-transform onset detection, IEEE Asilomar 2018), extended through a PhD and postdoc building streaming DSP feature pipelines, RNN-based sequence models, and edge inference (14~ms on Raspberry Pi~4, F1 0.76, 74x faster than the DTW baseline it replaced). Direct multi-sensor fusion experience combining inertial (IMU) and audio streams via hierarchical state machines (F1 0.78, McGill IDMIL/CIRMMT). Comfortable owning a signal-processing pipeline end to end -- acquisition, feature engineering, model training/evaluation, embedded deployment, and benchmarking -- and translating it across research and engineering audiences.
