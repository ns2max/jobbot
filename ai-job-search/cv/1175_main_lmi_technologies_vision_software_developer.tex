\section*{Professional Summary}
Machine learning engineer with a 5.5-year track record building production computer-vision and IoT
systems for South Asia's largest apparel manufacturer, followed by a PhD and postdoc in real-time,
embedded ML. At MAS Holdings, owned end-to-end industrial vision pipelines - camera and sensor
streams into conveyor and PLC-driven inspection lines - cutting inspection time by up to 99.5\% and
operator headcount by 50\%. That work included a camera-captured-garment-geometry-to-warp alignment
algorithm, a 2D geometric registration and image-warping problem directly adjacent to the 3D-to-2D
projection and calibration challenges underlying structured-light and multi-camera 3D scanning. PhD
research since has centred on real-time, resource-constrained perception (C++/Python, embedded
Linux, ARM), with published latency and accuracy benchmarks. Looking to bring that combination of
factory-floor CV delivery and geometric image processing to industrial 3D machine-vision sensors.
