\section*{Professional Summary}
ML research engineer whose core strength is rigorous evaluation methodology applied to real-time systems: designing frozen-protocol benchmark suites and ablation studies that quantify accuracy, latency, and compute trade-offs, and keeping a written record of negative results rather than only positive ones. This discipline produced a published real-time detector (14 ms inference on Raspberry Pi 4, F1 0.76, 74x faster than the DTW baseline it replaced) and MIRaaS, a UDP edge-to-server offload architecture whose accompanying paper characterizes the latency trade-offs of that design decision -- the same kind of systems-architecture reasoning a Principal Scientist role is asked to apply to production decision-making at scale. Owns full ML pipelines end to end, from data acquisition through training, evaluation, edge deployment, and monitoring. No supply-chain or operations-research domain experience to date, and early-career post-PhD rather than at principal/architect tenure; brings transferable architecture and evaluation rigor to a team that can provide the domain context.
