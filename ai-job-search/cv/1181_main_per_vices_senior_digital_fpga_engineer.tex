\section*{Professional Summary}
Signal-processing engineer with a PhD-level background in time-frequency analysis and 5+ years of embedded/hardware systems engineering. MSc thesis and a peer-reviewed IEEE Asilomar paper on S-transform-based extraction of transient events from noisy, continuous real-world signals -- the same time-frequency discipline that underlies digital signal chains in RF/SDR systems. Postdoctoral work at the University of Trento shipped a real-time detector running at 14 ms inference on Raspberry Pi 4 (F1 0.76, 31.4\% CPU, 74x faster than the DTW baseline it replaced), and built a UDP edge-to-server offload architecture for latency-characterized signal transmission. Direct hardware integration experience spans PLC interfacing on manufacturing lines, IMU/I2C sensor buses, and audio ADC/DAC hardware (HiFiBerry) on embedded Linux. No FPGA/HDL or RF-specific design experience to date; brings strong DSP theory, C++17 systems engineering, and a track record of taking signal-processing pipelines from algorithm to deployed hardware.

\textbf{Embedded Hardware Integration:} Raspberry Pi and ARM CPU profiling, embedded Linux deployment, IMU/I2C sensor integration, PLC interfacing (conveyor/reject systems), audio ADC/DAC hardware bring-up (HiFiBerry), real-time inference under hard latency and compute budgets.
