\section*{Research Interests}
Real-time and low-latency machine learning on embedded and edge hardware; time-series and sequential pattern detection; audio signal processing and music information retrieval; multimodal sensing (audio, IMU, EEG, mixed-reality telemetry); smart musical instruments and the Internet of Musical Things; benchmark and dataset design; human-centred evaluation of ML-driven interactive systems.

\section*{Academic Appointments}
\entry{Postdoctoral Researcher}{Jan 2025 -- Dec 2025}{CIMIL, University of Trento, Italy \textbar{} EU Horizon EIC Pathfinder project MUSMET (grant 101184379)}
\begin{cvlist}
  \item Owned the full ML pipeline for streaming audio and IMU/gesture data: acquisition, DSP and feature engineering, training and evaluation, edge deployment (Raspberry Pi 4, Elk Audio OS, VST, OSC) and monitoring.
  \item Published system detects audio patterns in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4 (F1 0.76) against 1038 ms for a DTW baseline; real-time inference under 30 ms on edge-class devices.
  \item Designed benchmark suites (baselines and ablations: RNN vs.\ DTW, audio vs.\ MIDI, pattern-set sizes 1/3/10) to quantify accuracy, latency and CPU trade-offs.
  \item Built a real-time multimodal extraction and synchronization pipeline (audio, EEG/BCI, head and hand tracking from mixed-reality headsets) across four musicians simultaneously, with SAE Institute Barcelona and g.tec medical engineering.
  \item Co-designed and ran two live multisensory concerts (20 audience members, 6 performers) used as a user study.
  \item Supervised two BSc theses (see Supervision). Four peer-reviewed outputs in 2025.
\end{cvlist}
\entry{Visiting Researcher}{May 2024 -- Aug 2024}{IDMIL / CIRMMT, McGill University, Montreal, Canada. Host: Prof.\ Marcelo M.\ Wanderley}
\begin{cvlist}
  \item Built a self-powered smart electric guitar (BNO055 IMU, HiFiBerry, Raspberry Pi 4) with real-time gesture detection (LSTM) fused with audio pattern detection through a hierarchical finite state machine; F1 0.78 on a 500-event corpus.
  \item Case study with five professional guitarists; compute and power profiling for edge feasibility; explored diffusion- and VAE-based synthetic data for low-label regimes.
\end{cvlist}
\entry{Visiting Researcher}{Jan 2024 -- Mar 2024}{University of Visual and Performing Arts, Colombo, Sri Lanka}
