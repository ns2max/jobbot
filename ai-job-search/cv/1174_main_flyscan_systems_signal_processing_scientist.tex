\section*{Professional Summary}
Signal processing scientist with a PhD in Information Engineering \& Computer Science (University of Trento) and an MSc thesis (Sheffield Hallam University, published IEEE Asilomar 2018) applying the Stockwell (S-)transform -- a time-frequency signal-processing technique -- to extract reliable transient events from noisy, continuous real-world signals. Postdoctoral work at Trento extended this into full DSP feature-engineering pipelines (STFT, MFCC, onset/beat tracking) with rigorous, frozen-protocol benchmarking of accuracy/latency/CPU trade-offs, culminating in a published real-time detector running at 14 ms on embedded ARM hardware (Raspberry Pi 4), 74x faster than the DTW baseline it replaced. Five and a half years of prior industry experience (MAS Holdings) building and deploying C++/Python signal- and image-processing systems on embedded and PLC-integrated hardware at manufacturing scale. Comfortable moving between algorithm design, benchmark rigor, and low-latency embedded deployment -- the same skill set condition-monitoring and partial-discharge signal extraction demands.
