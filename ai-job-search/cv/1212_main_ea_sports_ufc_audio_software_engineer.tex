\section*{Professional Summary}
C++ audio engineer with a PhD in real-time embedded audio systems, specializing in interactive, reactive sound under hard latency budgets. Built and shipped a real-time audio pattern-detection engine running at 14 ms inference on a Raspberry Pi 4 (F1 0.76, 31.4\% CPU) - 74x faster than the DTW baseline it replaced - published in the Journal of the Audio Engineering Society. Author of Nebula, an open-source C++17 audio-feature-extraction library with per-feature ARM/Raspberry Pi latency benchmarks. Deep hands-on experience with JUCE, Elk Audio OS, VST, OSC and MIDI, plus DSP fundamentals (STFT, MFCC, onset/beat tracking, real-time audio I/O and buffering) that translate directly to dynamic, event-driven mixing and reactive sound design for a live sports title. 15+ years as a performing and session guitarist (Trinity College London Rock \& Pop Guitar Grade 8) give a trained ear for audio quality alongside the engineering.
