\textbf{Re: \clRole{}, Autonomy \& Algorithms - \clCompany{}}

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am applying for the \clRole{} role at \clCompany{}, at the Senior level. Most of my career has been perception work: turning camera and multi-sensor streams into decisions that hold up on deployed hardware. At MAS Holdings I shipped production computer vision for manufacturing, including the camera-to-print geometry alignment behind Promptly, now a commercial on-demand printing product with facilities in four countries. In my PhD and postdoc at the University of Trento I took deep learning detectors for streaming sensor data from prototype to device, reaching 14~ms inference on a Raspberry Pi~4 at F1 0.76, 74x faster than the DTW baseline (JAES 2026).

What draws me to \clCompany{} is that a Toronto-based team is putting research into commercial service: customer operations with Volvo in Texas began this month, and the same end-to-end model is meant to serve both trucks and robotaxis. Waabi World, the company's closed-loop simulator, ties real-world data, simulation and rigorous evaluation together, which is how I already work.

Three parts of the role match what I have done:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=0.1em, topsep=0.1em]
    \item \textbf{Multimodal sensor fusion.} At McGill I fused an IMU gesture detector (LSTM) with audio pattern detection through a hierarchical state machine, F1 0.78 on 500 events (IEEE IS2 2025). On the EU MUSMET project I built a real-time pipeline that time-synchronizes audio, EEG and mixed-reality head/hand tracking from four performers at once.
    \item \textbf{Rigorous experimental validation.} I design benchmark suites and ablations before I trust a model (RNN vs.\ DTW, audio vs.\ MIDI, set sizes 1/3/10), published four open datasets with 9,800+ recordings, and recently ran a 100,000-trial benchmark of six scoring methods.
    \item \textbf{Production-quality code.} Python and C++17 are my primary languages; I ran C++/Python inference engines on embedded hardware, and introduced CI/CD and code review at MAS.
\end{itemize}

I have not yet driven projects on 3D detection or tracking of traffic agents, and I have not worked with LiDAR or self-driving data. What transfers directly is camera geometry and registration from Promptly, temporal state over noisy sensor streams, and a habit of proving a model on held-out real data before it ships. In my first months I would learn the domain through Waabi's datasets and simulator while contributing evaluation and fusion tooling to the perception stack.

I am a Canadian permanent resident based in \clCity{}, eligible to work without sponsorship, and available immediately for onsite, hybrid or remote work. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my experience in real-time perception and sensor fusion can support \clTeam{}. My publications and open datasets are at \href{\clSite}{nishal.xyz}.
