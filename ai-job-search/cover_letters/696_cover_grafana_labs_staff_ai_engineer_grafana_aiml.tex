What I would bring to AI features for incident detection, triage and resolution:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=0pt, topsep=2pt]

    \item \textbf{Fast experiments with honest evaluation.} At Trento I owned a streaming ML pipeline from acquisition to deployment and monitoring, and used benchmark suites and ablations to reach 14 ms inference at F1 0.76, 74x faster than the baseline (JAES 2026).     \item \textbf{Hands-on generative AI.} I LoRA fine-tuned MusicGen-medium with a custom conditioning head across two GPUs, benchmarked Wav2Vec2 transformer embeddings for pronunciation scoring over 100,000 trials, and build daily with Claude Code as an agentic workflow.

What draws me to Grafana Labs is Grafana Assistant Investigations, which forms hypotheses about an incident and proves or disproves each one against the data. In my first months I would expect to pair agent-based investigation workflows with the time-series anomaly detection and evaluation discipline I already practice, so each AI feature is measured against real incidents rather than demos.
