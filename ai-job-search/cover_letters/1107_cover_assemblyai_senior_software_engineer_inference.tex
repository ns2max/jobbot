I am applying for the Senior Software Engineer, Inference role at AssemblyAI in Toronto. I am an engineer and researcher who turns audio ML research into running, low-latency systems, with a PhD from the University of Trento and more than ten years in C++ and Python across industry and academia. Most recently I built MIRaaS, an edge-to-server architecture that offloads audio analysis over the network, and characterized its end-to-end latency for near-real-time use (IEEE IS\textsuperscript{2} 2026). That is the same shape of problem as taking a model capability from proof of concept to an API customers can depend on.

The role sits between research and the Applied teams, productionizing new techniques and scaling inference. My work has lived on both sides of that line. I have designed model architectures and the benchmark and ablation suites that turn a research question into measured latency, CPU and accuracy trade-offs, and I have shipped C++ and Python inference engines on production hardware across MAS Holdings factories on three continents, processing millions of events per day with CI/CD, code review and monitoring that I put in place. I have taken more than one computer-vision prototype from a notebook to a production line with hard reliability requirements. What I have not done is operate an API at the scale of a million users, and that is the part I would be learning, on top of a solid base in service design, streaming I/O and profiling.

AssemblyAI is a compelling place to do this. Universal-3.5 Pro Realtime ships configurable latency modes that let customers tune speed against accuracy, which is exactly the trade-off my research quantifies, and the Voice Agent API collapsing speech, language and speech synthesis into one streaming path is the kind of latency-sensitive system I most enjoy building. A lean team where one engineer owns a capability end to end is how I do my best work. In the first months I would take ownership of one or two model-backed APIs from prototype through launch and iteration, and bring measurement discipline to where latency is being spent.

I am a Canadian permanent resident based in Toronto, eligible to work without sponsorship and available immediately, so this role needs no relocation. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience turning audio research into production systems can support \clTeam{} at AssemblyAI. My portfolio, publications and open datasets are at \href{\clSite}{nishal.xyz}.
