## Overview
Nishal presents as a **builder / tinkerer** pattern: self-directed, hands-on, and happiest owning a
problem end to end, from hardware and firmware through the ML model to a deployed, monitored system.
He has spent ~10 years alternating between industry (MAS Holdings, Forestpin) and research (Trento,
McGill), and moves comfortably between deep technical work and stakeholder-facing translation.
