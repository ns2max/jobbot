\section*{Professional Summary}

Machine Learning Engineer with 10+ years across industry and academia, including 6 years building production ML systems in industry. I build machine learning pipelines end to end: data extraction, data mining, feature development, deep learning model training, testing, deployment, and monitoring of deployed models against the metrics that matter to the product. My work spans time-series and sequence modelling, computer vision at manufacturing scale, and anomaly scoring on large structured datasets. Published results include real-time detection in 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4 at F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the baseline (JAES 2026); industrial computer-vision systems cut inspection time by 99.5\% and lifted throughput by up to 300\%. I evaluate models with baselines, ablations, hypothesis tests, and evaluation metrics such as ROC-AUC and F1, at the scale of 100,000 scored trials.
