\section*{Professional Summary}
Computer vision and machine learning engineer with 10+ years across industry and applied research, turning camera and multi-sensor streams into real-time decisions on deployed hardware. At MAS Holdings, shipped production computer vision for manufacturing at scale, including the camera-captured geometry alignment behind Promptly, now a commercial on-demand printing product, and care-label inspection that cut inspection time by 99.5\%. Through a PhD and postdoc, took deep learning models from research prototype to deployment: 14~ms inference on a Raspberry Pi~4 at F1 0.76, 74x faster than the DTW baseline (JAES 2026), and multimodal sensor fusion of IMU and audio at F1 0.78 (IEEE IS2 2025). Rigorous experimental validation (benchmarks, ablations, open datasets), tested Python and C++, and 10+ peer-reviewed publications; now bringing this toolkit to self-driving perception.
