I am applying for the \clRole{} role on \clTeam{} at \clCompany{}. You need someone who can take a model from idea to production and keep it healthy. Here is how my work maps to that:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=0.1em, topsep=0.2em]
    \item \textbf{Risk models on structured data.} At Forestpin I designed and deployed ML and statistical pipelines for financial forensics, compliance monitoring and risk detection on large structured enterprise datasets, and built the SQL + Python scoring services that flagged anomalies for triage in production.
    \item \textbf{Low-latency decision systems.} On the EU-funded MUSMET project I owned the full pipeline, from feature engineering to training, deployment and monitoring, with inference held under 30 ms. At MAS Holdings I shipped live accept/reject inspection (OpenCV with PLC reject) that cut inspection time by 99.5\%.
    \item \textbf{Offline experiments before production.} I design baselines and ablations to choose what ships, and recently benchmarked 6 scoring methods over 100,000 trials for a binary classifier (best ROC-AUC 0.822 vs.\ 0.599 baseline).
    \item \textbf{Engineering practice.} I introduced CI/CD and code review at MAS, mentored engineers, orchestrate pipelines in Airflow, and use Claude Code daily as part of my development workflow.
\end{itemize}
To be direct about the gaps: gradient-boosted tree libraries (LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost) and Spark are not yet in my daily toolkit, and I have not modelled transaction graphs. My structured-data scoring at Forestpin and my Python, scikit-learn and PyTorch work make these a fast ramp rather than a restart.

Affirm's FY2026 annual report describes fraud models that draw on roughly 350 data points to make a near-instantaneous decision on whether to block a transaction, at a scale of \$50.2 billion in GMV. That is the problem I want to work on: feature-rich models under a hard latency budget, tuned as a balance between fraud loss, customer experience and conversion. In my first months I would aim to own one model end to end, from features and offline backtests to a monitored production rollout.

I am a Canadian permanent resident living in Ontario, eligible to work without sponsorship, available immediately, and glad to attend in-person onboarding. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience in real-time ML and risk scoring can support \clTeam{} at \clCompany{}.
