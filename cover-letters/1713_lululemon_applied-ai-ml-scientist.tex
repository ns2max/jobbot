Applying ML to apparel and turning it into something operations teams rely on is work I know well.

For time-series, computer vision and multimodal modeling, I built real-time pipelines for sensor and camera streams at MAS, and at Trento I time-synchronized audio, EEG and head and hand tracking from four musicians at once. I am also fine-tuning an audio foundation model with LoRA, and I have worked with Python, PyTorch and AWS (SageMaker, S3, ECS).

I should be direct about the gaps. I have not built demand forecasting or personalization models on retail data, and I have not run online A/B tests that measure business impact at enterprise scale. My evaluation work has been offline benchmarks and user studies. Much of my industry background is in manufacturing, and my cloud ML work has been lighter than my research. The underlying methods are the same, and I expect to learn your data and experimentation stack quickly. What draws me to lululemon is applied research that has to ship and be judged on real-world performance, with product and engineering teams working side by side.

I am a Canadian permanent resident based in Toronto, eligible to work without sponsorship, and available immediately.
