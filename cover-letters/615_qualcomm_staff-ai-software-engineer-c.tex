My mathematics-for-ML foundation is strong: DSP, vectorized numerical computation, and an electronic engineering background (BEng/MSc) that makes processor architecture familiar territory.

I note the team does not train models, and that suits me. My strength is the inference and systems side: taking other people's models and making them run brilliantly on real hardware, with the profiling discipline and low-level debugging that on-device support demands. Qualcomm's silicon is where that work matters most, and I would expect to be productive on runtime optimization and operator-level performance within the first few months.
