I have built the same pieces with other tools, and I expect to pick up your stack quickly. What draws me to ACV is that the models serve a high-volume marketplace, where precision, recall and throughput all matter and the work is judged by outcomes. Within the first months I would aim to contribute to damage detection quality and serving latency.
