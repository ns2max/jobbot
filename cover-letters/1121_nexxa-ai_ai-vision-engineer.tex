Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the \clRole{} position. Production computer vision for heavy industry, across the full stack, classical and deep learning, running in real time at the edge and in the cloud, is exactly the work I did for five years at MAS Holdings and sharpened during my PhD. My published detector runs at 14 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4, F1 0.76, 74$\times$ faster than the baseline (JAES 2026).

At MAS I built and deployed camera CV across manufacturing lines: automated label inspection with OpenCV template and feature matching and an explainable overlay that cut inspection time 99.5\% and raised throughput 300\%; a real-time microscopic camera array detecting fabric defects at the loom that cut operators 50\%; and the geometry-alignment algorithm plus RIP integration for an on-demand printing product now deployed across four countries. That work ran next to the camera on constrained hardware and tied into PLC-driven machinery, so algorithm choices had to respect the system, not just the benchmark. My research added the discipline: profiling feature extraction, architecture and OS scheduling to hit latency budgets, and quantifying every trade-off with baselines and ablations.

On vision-language models and multimodal reasoning I am earlier in the curve, my multimodal work has been audio, IMU and sensor fusion rather than image-and-text, but the fusion instinct transfers (a hierarchical fusion state machine got me F1 0.78 on a 500-event corpus), and my cross-domain record, repurposing SSIM from computer vision into an award-shortlisted audio method, is evidence I move methods across domains fast. The classical-plus-deep vision stack and real-time edge deployment are where I am strongest, and that is the core of this role.

I am a Canadian permanent resident based in Toronto, eligible to work without sponsorship, and available immediately. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my production industrial CV and edge-inference experience can support \clCompany{}.
