## C. Discovery — Employment Hero (CA)

**How the board works:** `employmenthero.com/en-ca/jobs/` renders fine in a browser, and each job page carries JSON-LD. **Query "ml", Canada, radius 25 returns only 14 jobs on one page** (`page_index=2` is empty, so there is no page 3). Almost every result is a **CareerBeacon syndication** (slugs `cb-…` / `careerbeacon-demo-…`) that redirects to the employer's own ATS, and the JSON-LD has **no `validThrough`**. Closing dates below therefore come from the job text or the employer's Workday `endDate`. "On-site" is Employment Hero's default tag and is unreliable, so the work arrangement comes from the JD or employer ATS where stated. "ml" only matches literal "ML" in titles, so I also ran "machine learning" (pages 1–4; relevant hits stop after page 2), "computer vision", "signal processing", "audio", "data scientist" and "artificial intelligence". Relevant hits from those are included.
